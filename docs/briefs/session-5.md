## 1. New design direction (first, everything else is built in it)

Replace section 9 of `CLAUDE.md` and of `AGENTS.md` with the direction below (keep the level shapes and the four level colours), record it in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, then apply it to every existing page.

**Professional spaces look like an operations console.**
- A fixed left sidebar in deep ink (`#0B1A16`), with the logo, the role's name and organisation ("Pharmacie démo A, Tunis" with its Simulé badge), the space's sections as navigation items with icons, and "Changer de rôle" at the bottom. On phones it becomes a bottom tab bar (pharmacy) or a top drawer (other roles).
- A light content area (`#F5F7F6` page, white panels) laid out on a grid: map and key figures at the top, then the working lists.
- A compact strip of key figures at the top of each dashboard: a label, the value, a 14-day sparkline, all computed. No decorative giant numbers.
- Panels are allowed for grouping (white, 1 px `--line` border, 8 px radius, no shadow or a very soft one). Not a box around every element: lists and tables inside a panel stay separated by hairlines.
- Live feel: when a level changes, the governorate on the map and the row in the list pulse once; a small toast says what changed. Transitions of 150 to 250 ms. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Charts where they carry information, drawn with one small library (record it in `DECISIONS.md`): a level timeline per medicine (a coloured band over 30 days), reports per day, patient demand per day, a split before/after for wholesalers.

**The pharmacy space and the public pages feel like a mobile app.**
- At 360 px: an app header, a bottom tab bar (pharmacy: Signaler, Mes médicaments, Alertes, Plus), large search field, rows you can tap with the whole width, a clear primary action fixed at the bottom of a flow.
- The report flow keeps its two taps, with a progress line and a satisfying confirmation (the level, what your report changed, a short animation of the count).
- The web app manifest from session 3 makes it installable: add the "Ajouter à l'écran d'accueil" hint on the pharmacy space.

**Identity.**
- A logo mark of our own, drawn in SVG (a simple, original shape; no cross, no pill cliché, no institution's symbol). Used in the sidebar, the favicon, the share cards.
- One icon set, used everywhere (Lucide or similar, record the dependency).
- IBM Plex Sans and Plex Mono stay. One text style per sentence stays.

**Still never:** cream or beige backgrounds, orange-and-blue pairings, gradients as decoration, glass effects, emoji as icons, stock illustrations, italic or coloured words inside a heading.

**The home page shows the product, not a text.**
- Top: the live national picture. The Tunisia map filled by the most serious active level per governorate, the list of medicines with a level next to it, and one line: "Repérer les ruptures de médicaments avant qu'elles n'atteignent le comptoir." Every figure computed, every simulated item badged.
- Then "Choisissez un rôle", grouped: Terrain (Pharmacie Tunis, Pharmacie Sfax, Pharmacie hospitalière, Grossiste), Pilotage (Ministère, Pharmacie centrale, Ordre des pharmaciens), Public (Je cherche un médicament, Médecin). Each role is a tile with its icon, one line on what it does, and a small preview of its screen (a static screenshot of that space, regenerated by a script, not drawn by hand).
- Then the scenario button, then "Comprendre" (scenario, four views, method, replay).

Done when every existing page has moved to this direction at 360 and 1280 px, Lighthouse accessibility stays at 100, and `npm run check` passes.

## 5. The institutions get their own space

Labels say "conçu pour", never "utilisé par". Demo organisations are fictional and badged. No logo of any institution. Rule 1 holds in every space: counts by governorate, `<3` under 3, no pharmacy name.

**Ministère de la Santé, cellule nationale** (the existing coordinator role and `/coordination`, renamed and upgraded; keep the route and the tests):
- Key figures: medicines by level, vital medicines confirmed, governorates affected, patient requests unanswered in 7 days.
- The national map, filterable by level; the medicines list sorted by level, then by CNAM category (vital first), then by how many other brands exist (fewer first).
- Per medicine: the existing panel, plus the level timeline and the reports and demand charts.
- "Note de situation": a printable one-page brief (print stylesheet, opens the browser's print to PDF), built only from computed values and fixed sentences with blanks: date, medicines by level, the confirmed ones with their governorates count, wholesaler decisions of the week, unanswered demand. No free text from you.
- The export and the decision log stay.

**Pharmacie centrale** (new organisation type `central_pharmacy`, route `/centrale`, demo account "Centrale d'achat démo, Tunis"):
- The upstream view: every foreign notice with its regulator and link, and for each, whether the molecule has a level in Tunisia.
- Risk list: molecules at any level, with their CNAM category and how many other CNAM brands exist ("aucune autre marque" stands out). Both computed from the catalogue.
- Action: for a medicine at "probable" or "confirmée", record "Arrivage annoncé" with an expected date and a quantity band (choose from "moins d'un mois de besoins", "un à trois mois", "plus de trois mois"). Saved with its origin in a new table with row-level security; logged in the decision log.
- The announcement is then shown to pharmacies on the medicine's page and in their alert, and on the public status of a confirmed medicine as "Arrivage annoncé par la centrale d'achat pour le <date>", with its badge. It never changes a level.

**Ordre des pharmaciens** (new organisation type `pharmacists_order`, route `/ordre`, demo account "Conseil régional démo, Sfax"):
- The network: member pharmacies by governorate (counts, `<3` rule), active in the last 30 days, reports per week. This is the participation picture, not any pharmacy's activity.
- Invite pharmacies: the existing tester invitation, from this space.
- "Message aux pharmaciens": the Order writes a short professional message (for example on a confirmed shortage), choosing the governorates; pharmacies there see it in their alerts with the sender, its badge and "Ceci n'est pas une déclaration officielle de rupture." Stored in a new table with row-level security. The Order sees no report and no pharmacy name.

Add the three demo accounts to `lib/demo/roles.ts` and `supabase/seed/demo-organisations.json`, with access tests per table like session 3's, and update `/vues` to show the new roles' panels.

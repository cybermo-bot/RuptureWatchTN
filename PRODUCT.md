# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Patients in Tunisia who need to check a medicine or ask nearby pharmacies whether a presentation is available.
- Pharmacists at the counter who report missed deliveries and reply to availability requests.
- Wholesalers who review shortage signals and compare ways to allocate a delivery.
- Coordinators and institutional users who review signals, supporting evidence and decisions.

## Product Purpose

RuptureWatch TN is a demonstration of how Tunisia could detect medicine shortages earlier and coordinate a response. A visitor should see how an undelivered order becomes a report, how reports change a signal, and who can act.

## Operating Context

Patients enter through the public medicine search and use the service anonymously. Pharmacies and wholesalers have separate sign-in paths. Ministry and pharmacists' order access is issued directly by the project team. Demo roles use fictional organisations and are reached from the scenario.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use the tested evaluator for signals and the existing allocation functions. Carry the selected catalogue presentation in a patient request. Use visible origin labels, suppress small counts and show only confirmed or cleared status on public medicine pages. Follow the full privacy and clinical text rules in AGENTS.md.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use French and Arabic on patient surfaces, including right-to-left layout. Design for a 360 px mobile viewport, keyboard access, WCAG AA contrast and reduced motion.
